Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Zoro Platform operates a multi-role ecosystem connecting Customers, Store Owners (Business Partners), and Delivery Partners (Riders). This policy details the specific data we collect for each role to ensure a safe, efficient, and transparent delivery experience. This policy applies to our Zoro App and Zoro Rider applications.

1. Detailed Information Collection

A. For Customer Accounts ("Zoro App")

Identity & Contact Info Name, Phone Number and Email Address.
Reason: Account security, order updates, and rider communication.
Delivery Locations Saved addresses, address labels, and Precise GPS Coordinates.
Reason: Accurate order delivery and identifying nearby stores.
Shopping History Order details, basket contents, and favorite products/stores.
Reason: Personalized experience and order record keeping.

B. For Business Accounts ("Zoro App")

Business Profile Store name, description, category and marketing image (Banner).
Reason: Displaying your business to platform users.
Verification Documents Business license photos, storefront images and identity verification docs.
Reason: Ensuring marketplace legitimacy and safety.
Financial Details Bank Account Number, IFSC Code, Account Holder Name and UPI ID.
Reason: Processing automated earnings payouts to your bank.
Operational Data Business hours, contact links (WhatsApp/Instagram) and product inventory.
Reason: Managing order flow and customer inquiries.

C. For Delivery Partners ("Zoro Rider")

Rider Identity Full name, Phone number, Email, and Profile Picture.
Reason: Professional verification and customer recognition.
Vehicle Information Vehicle type (e.g., Bike, Truck) and Registration Number.
Reason: Matching load capacity with orders and legal compliance.
Financial Payout Info UPI ID for earnings settlement.
Reason: Facilitating swift daily/weekly earnings distribution.

Continuous Location Tracking (Background)

To ensure real-time order tracking for customers and to optimize order assignments, the Zoro Rider app collects precise location data even when the app is in the background or closed. This tracking is active only when you are "Online" or on an "Active Delivery".

D. Automatically Collected Data (All Users)

Device Identifiers FCM Notification Tokens, device model, and OS version.
Reason: Delivering critical order alerts and technical optimization.
Interaction Logs App usage patterns, search queries, and crash reports.
Reason: Platform troubleshooting and service improvement.

2. Data Safety & Security

Data Protection All data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption via Supabase. We use SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit between our apps and servers.
Data Sharing We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with parties necessary for the delivery process (e.g., sharing a customer's address with the assigned rider) or with trusted service providers like Google Firebase for notifications.

3. Children's Privacy

Zoro Platform is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal info, we will delete it immediately from our servers.

4. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

To request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, please use one of the following methods:

  • In-App: Go to Settings > Profile > Delete Account (if available).
  • Email: Send a request to zorodeliveryapp@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

We will process your request and purge your data from our active records within 30 days, subject to legal or regulatory retention requirements.
